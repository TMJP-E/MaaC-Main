*The Foundations* introduces the main building blocks, ideas and mindset with which we will learn and build mathematics, they are, in themselves, math too, but they enable us to construct our first big idea, counting, they will be reviewed in more detail on the future, when it occurs we need a broader understanding.foundations.
1. [Philosophy, Science and Ideas](11-PhilosophyScienceIdeas): The mindset that one requires to learn math adequately, what abstractions and ideas are.
2. [The language of math](13-MathLanguage.md): Definitions, terminology and symbols that help us simplify our writing.
3. [Preamble on Logic](12-LogicPreamble.md)[^1]: Mathematics rely deeply on the concepts that make up a certain system of logic we need to be familiar with.
4. [How to count](14-HowToCount): A small narrative that serves as a thought experiment, showing how one creates math and models life around it.
5. [Results, definitions, terms.](15-Results).
[^1]: Logic is another complex subject we won't discuss in full detail, only the necessary amount to understand how it can be used in constructing math.
