## General Notation

Common symbols, syntax, and semantics used all throughout math, based on objects and relation.

| Symbol | Meaning | Example |
| :--: | :--- | :--: |
| $A,b,Y,x,\mathbb{R},\mathbf{I},\mathit{p},\mathcal{H},\mathscr{F},\mathfrak{Z},\delta,\dots$ | Any letter of the English or Greek alphabet, they can represent variables, operators, constants, sets, and many more, however, they are usually just used as objects and variables. | $\alpha,\beta,\omega,\Gamma,\epsilon,\varphi,\Omega,\dots$ |
| $=$ | Equality, the objects are the same, this notion can be primitive or well-defined, depending on the area of study. | $a=b$ |
